## Patterns of Papers

### Paradigm Lost
Unexpected facts challenge prevailing models and prompt a reassessment.

### Measure for Measure
New data, methods, or assumptions make previously elusive concepts measurable.

### Usual Suspects
A familiar puzzle gains a simple explanation, or new evidence overturns a common belief.

### Timely Insights
Analysis of emerging trends reveals what matters now, including hidden risks.

### After the Fact
An event or policy change motivates an account of its causes and consequences.
