Most uses of [AI as a Tool]({{ "/tool/overview/" | relative_url }}) are variable costs: one prompt per task. This tab covers the fixed costs: work done once that every later task uses. AI reproduces the patterns you give it, so each fixed cost is an investment in good patterns.

1. [Data]({{ "/infrastructure/data/" | relative_url }}): a repository of papers, with summaries and full extracted text, and a data manual with written rules and checklists.
2. [Code]({{ "/infrastructure/code/" | relative_url }}): multiple agents checking each other, with one as drafter and another as critic.
3. [Taste]({{ "/infrastructure/taste/" | relative_url }}): a written statement of what makes a good paper, so the model judges ideas the way I would.

The fixed costs pay off only with heavy use. For occasional use, the six uses in AI as a Tool are enough.
