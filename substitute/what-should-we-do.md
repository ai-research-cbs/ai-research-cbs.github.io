## Invest in What Differentiates You as a Researcher

1. Unique access to data and research settings (“access”)
2. Relationships and reputation (“trust”)
3. Communication and storytelling (“voice”)
4. Likeability, humor, and charisma (“aura”)
5. Unique institutional knowledge (“depth”)
6. Initiative and Execution (“agency”)

## Shift Effort from Producing Answers to Choosing Qs and Checking As

<ol start="7">
  <li>Identifying important, original questions (“taste”)</li>
  <li>Connecting ideas across fields (“range”)</li>
</ol>

## Spend Less Time on Work You Can Reliably Delegate

<ol start="9"><li>Research infrastructure (“scale”)</li></ol>

## Complementary Skills

1. Cultivate a brand for yourself: be collegial, accumulate interesting experiences.
2. Accumulate breadth of knowledge: expands the set of questions you can ask.
3. Develop a taste for quality: AI reproduces patterns; you decide which patterns are good.
