This tab asks whether AI will do research in place of researchers. It has three parts.

1. [Narrative]({{ "/substitute/narrative/" | relative_url }}): the claim that AI advances in math are coming to economics, and why math moved first. Proofs have a cheap, reliable verifier.
2. [Exposure]({{ "/substitute/exposure/" | relative_url }}): research is not one thing. Seven goals of a paper differ in whether a verifier exists and in what limits AI, so they differ in their exposure to AI.
3. [What should we do?]({{ "/substitute/what-should-we-do/" | relative_url }}): invest in what differentiates you, shift effort from producing answers to choosing questions and checking answers, and spend less time on work you can reliably delegate.
